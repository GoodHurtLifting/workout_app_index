# Initial-v1 scoring rule

The five Legit Score criteria are equally weighted (20% each). Each criterion uses five-point increments; the displayed total is their rounded arithmetic mean. Use the same anchors for every app: 90-100 means demonstrated, consistently strong performance; 75-89 means strong observed or documented performance with limited issues; 60-74 means a credible offering with material observed issues; 40-59 means major observed limitations; below 40 means core failure or substantial negative evidence. An untested feature is not treated as working, but a missing long-term test is not itself negative evidence. It lowers confidence, not the numeric score. Compare candidates against the privately reassessed legacy entries under this rubric, not unrevised public legacy scores.

The criteria are: core execution (does the main training/logging promise work?), usability (can a person understand and complete the flow?), reliability (did the observed paths function without confirmed failures?), value (current free and paid boundaries relative to delivered utility), and support/privacy (published disclosures, visible controls, stated support, and any response WAI directly observed). The last criterion does not certify legal compliance, backend data handling, or security. Public product claims and store disclosures inform the assessment; direct testing is distinguished from those claims.

Originality & Identity uses five equally weighted dimensions already represented in the catalog: original mechanics, product point of view, visual identity, meaningful differentiation, and defensibility. Its total is their rounded mean. A feature's mere existence is not proof of originality. A low-confidence originality score can move after direct competitor and hands-on testing.

## Editorial boundary

Developer answers may correct facts, but they do not set WAI scores. The owner reviews the exact evidence-backed card before approval; publication is a separate action. Any paid private audit conversation is separate from these scores and from listing eligibility.
