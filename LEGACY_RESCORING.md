# Legacy catalog rescoring: private owner-review drafts

Checked October 9, 2026. These 36 apps were public before the owner-review workflow existed. Their old scores are not reliably comparable with the newer Aldo and Stark drafts: the original Legit totals had no recorded five-criterion breakdown, and some Originality totals did not equal their five dimensions. This pass creates a consistent private replacement for both scores. It does **not** revise the public scores, mark any app Reviewed, or publish a catalog snapshot.

Legit uses five equally weighted criteria: core execution, usability, reliability, value, and support/privacy. Originality uses original mechanics, product point of view, visual identity, meaningful differentiation, and defensibility. Totals are rounded means. See `RESEARCH_ALDO_STARK.md` for the shared scoring anchors. Every draft records a direct official or store source, check date, rationale, and confidence in `lib/legacy-score-drafts.ts`. A public claim can support a provisional assessment but cannot establish sustained reliability, verified privacy behavior, or exclusive originality. No draft has High confidence. The Lift League receives no special treatment for shared ownership; its distinctive scoring and identity are credited, while unverified defensibility and limited operating history constrain the draft.

## How to review and release

The admin editor overlays these drafts only for unreviewed legacy records that have no saved category assessment or owner-edited total. An owner-edited or Reviewed record is never overwritten. Opening a legacy card shows the proposed criteria, rationale, source, and a warning if the draft is not saved. Saving it while Researching keeps the revised score private. A changed legacy profile reaches visitors only after evidence is attached, the owner approves the exact version, it is marked Reviewed, and the approved catalog is published. Existing public legacy listings remain visible meanwhile.

Prioritize deeper checks for apps with Low confidence, changed prices or tiers, claims about adaptive coaching, and all support/privacy or reliability judgments. Recheck the official source and current store in the relevant country before accepting the score. For originality, compare real competitors and use the app before treating a distinctive mechanic or visual identity as established. This pass is an evidence-limited calibration, not a hands-on certification of 36 products.
