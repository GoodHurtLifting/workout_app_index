## Concise proposed research card

**Position / fit.** A goal-led wellness planner and tracker spanning strength, nutrition, activity, and recovery. Best for someone who values a shared daily checklist and basic tracking across those areas. A poor current fit for someone prioritizing detailed strength logging or dependable, low-friction food entry.

**Training relationship.** Goal setup removes some scheduling and target-setting decisions; the user still interprets ambiguous activity targets, checks the selected exercises, supplies loads, and manages food entries. The convenience is one coordinated daily view; the tradeoff is breadth with uneven specialist depth. Owner testing found useful day-to-day structure, not verified ongoing adaptation or a complete prescribed strength program.

**Practical facts.** iOS/iPadOS and Android have public listings. Official monthly USD offers are $2.99 basic and $4.99 Plus after seven-day trials; owner selected basic. Owner also saw Plus at $34.99/year and annual basic options, whose amount was not dictated. Official pages retrieved October 23 still describe monthly offers only: preserve the discrepancy. Explore allowed a demo preview, not a verified continuing free core tier. AI supports chat/recommendations and advertised food recognition; it is not proven necessary for ordinary logging. [Site](https://getswith.com/), [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swithai-workout-meal-log/id6783848103), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wellnesschampion.app).

**Distinctive experience / caveats.** Goal-to-task coordination and themed imagery make the breadth tangible. A workout log completed and correctly credited Goal, and a timed recovery flow reached its endpoint. Conversely, two branded-food attempts showed clipped serving controls and no visible nutrient scaling; repeated photo-entry attempts stalled. Upper Body Strength opened a checked selection containing squats and RDLs, and the encountered strength log used exercise-level summaries without apparent individual-set entry or a rest timer. Privacy disclosures still conflict with current health/GPS features. These are scoped Android observations, not universal claims.

**Proposed scores.** Legit **61/100**; Originality & Identity **63/100**, both provisional, overall **Low confidence**. Ten decision rows below retain positive evidence, counterevidence, and band reasoning. No public legacy score was used as a numerical comparator.

**Proposed public summary.** swithAI brings training, nutrition, activity and recovery into a goal-led daily plan. Android testing found polished presentation and useful task coordination, but a limited strength-logging flow and repeated problems with food portions and photo entry. Its breadth may appeal more than its depth to users seeking one place for daily wellness habits.

**Decision.** Ready for owner discussion as a qualified local research proposal. No more routine testing required. Before any saved change, obtain the exact current saved record and show Saved now versus Proposed field by field, including scores; owner approval and publication remain separate.
