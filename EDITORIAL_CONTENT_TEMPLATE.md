Effective November 4, 2026. Use alongside CATALOG_DICTATED_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md. This is a working template for the editor, not a form Ryan must fill out.

## Commission and consumer question

- Format: hands-on review / app-versus-app comparison / pricing or free-tier comparison / alternatives / best apps for a defined need / onboarding or paywall comparison / podcast / video.
- Consumer question, intended audience, and decision this piece helps them make:
- Selection evidence: dated actual search queries/impressions, comparison opportunity, uncertainty, or chosen editorial theme. Do not invent demand data.
- Products and selection criteria; explain comparison exclusions and any commercial relationship:
- Intended primary output and reuse: profile fact update, article, comparison, podcast outline, video demonstration, short clips. Each output retains the evidence limits.

## Natural dictated notes

Preserve Ryan's narration in sequence, with date/session markers. Ask only a targeted follow-up when a material point is unclear. A visible control is not proof that an action succeeds. Record attempts and actual results without guessing causes. Do not turn silence, an inaccessible tier, or a planned test into a conclusion. Notes are the primary evidence for firsthand experience.

Keep editorial inference in a separate reasoned paragraph with evidence IDs and comparison set. Do not relabel marketing claims as observed behavior. Price includes currency, billing period, annual-versus-monthly terms, trial conversion, free limits, and unknown checkout variants. Date the check; do not imply all countries see the same offer.

## Output-specific framing

- Hands-on review: tested journey, supported strengths and friction, intended user, untested areas, dated conclusion.
- App-versus-app: same consumer task, separate test scopes, meaningful tradeoffs, and asymmetric evidence limits.
- Pricing/free-tier: dated regional primary-source table; no claim of personally buying or testing unless observed.
- Alternatives: why someone seeks a replacement, comparable needs, and evidence-supported tradeoffs.
- Best apps: explicit audience, eligibility criteria, comparison set, evidence scope, and no universal winner or compensation-based placement.
- Onboarding/paywalls: attempted access path, trial conversion, purchase boundary, and what happened next; no inference of backend failure.
- Podcast/video: evidence-linked outline and demonstration plan, test date/version in source notes, accurate spoken limits, and one intended tracked action. Never fabricate footage, quotations, or a performed workout.

## Public draft and approval

Lead with the consumer decision. Include tested platform, available version, date, attempted workflows, direct observations, untested areas, relevant public sources, and important comparison limitations. For source-only pieces say there was no hands-on test. Disclose material commercial and Turf King ownership relationships. Reuse the same evidence across outputs without broadening its scope. Scores require the unchanged rubric and defensible evidence; do not add them merely to complete an article.

Record: local draft prepared / exact owner-approved version / separately published and verified. New public content remains unpublished until explicitly approved. Keep private chronology and contact details out of public copy and GitHub.
